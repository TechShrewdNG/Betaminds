+ Who can apply? Betaminds Africa Academy welcomes anyone passionate about
learning and building a successful career in the creative and digital industries. Our
programs are open to:
● Students and recent graduates
● Aspiring and established creatives
● Entrepreneurs and business owners
● Working professionals seeking to upskill or transition careers
● Freelancers and content creators
● Corporate teams looking for professional development
No matter your level of experience, we offer programs designed to help you grow
from where you are.
+ Are classes online? Yes. Betaminds Africa Academy offers flexible learning options
to suit different schedules and locations. Depending on the program, classes may be:
● Online (live instructor-led sessions)
● Self-paced online learning
● Physical classroom sessions
● Hybrid learning (a combination of online and in-person classes)
This flexibility allows participants from across Nigeria and Africa to learn conveniently.
+ How much is tuition? Tuition fees vary depending on the course, duration, and
learning format. We strive to keep our programs affordable while delivering
high-quality, industry-relevant training.
Please visit the course page for your selected program or contact our admissions
team for the latest tuition fees, available payment plans, scholarships, and
promotional offers.
+ Do you provide certificates? Yes. Participants who successfully complete their
course requirements, including attendance, assignments, and practical projects
where applicable, receive a Betaminds Africa Academy Certificate of Completion.

An initiative designed to empower aspiring individuals in rural areas,
empowering less privileged individuals, particularly those in orphanages and
motherless baby homes. to pursue careers in media and digital marketing.
We provide a comprehensive program that:
● Introduces them to creative industry career paths
● Offers free training and mentorship
● Equips them with the necessary tools and resources
Our goal is to unlock their innate potential, nurture their talents, and enable
them to achieve greatness in the creative industry, ultimately becoming
gainfully self-employed and fulfilled professionals.
This initiative will create a ripple effect, empowering a new generation of
leaders to pay it forward and inspire positive transformation in other young
individuals in rural areas, perpetuating a cycle of growth, development, and
community upliftment.
The movement aims to nurture future leaders, equipping them to transform their lives and
positively impact others from similar backgrounds. Within the next decade, envisions
building a community of over 1,000 independent digital experts across various specializations,
who are fully employed and self-sufficient.

BETAMINDS AFRICA CREATIVE EMPOWERMENT SUMMIT
The Betaminds Africa Creative Empowerment Summit is a premier platform
dedicated to empowering Africa’s next generation of creatives, innovators,
entrepreneurs, and young professionals. More than an annual event, the summit is a
movement committed to transforming creative talent into sustainable careers,
thriving businesses, lasting impact and contributing hugely to Social Corporate
Responsibility in education, innovation, and creativity.
Every year, we bring together industry leaders, successful entrepreneurs, creative
professionals, policymakers, investors, and aspiring talents for an immersive
experience of learning, inspiration, networking, and collaboration. Through keynote
sessions, panel discussions, masterclasses, workshops, mentorship, and career
opportunities, participants gain practical knowledge, valuable industry insights, and
meaningful connections that accelerate personal and professional growth.
Mission: To bridge the gap between talent and opportunity by equipping
participants with the skills, mindset, and networks needed to thrive in today’s
fast-evolving creative and digital economy. We believe that Africa’s greatest resource
is its people, and by investing in creativity, innovation, and entrepreneurship, we are
helping shape a future where young Africans create jobs, build globally competitive
businesses, and drive sustainable economic development.
